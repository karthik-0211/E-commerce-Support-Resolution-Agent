# PERISHABLE FOOD ITEMS POLICY
Last Updated: March 2024

## SECTION 1: PERISHABLE ITEMS DEFINITION
Perishable items include fresh produce, dairy products, meat and poultry, seafood, prepared meals, deli items, bakery goods, flowers and plants, and any items with expiration dates within 30 days. These items have special handling requirements and limited return windows due to food safety regulations.

## SECTION 2: QUALITY ISSUES AND REFUNDS
Spoiled or damaged perishable items require immediate reporting within 24 hours of delivery. Photographs must clearly show the issue and expiration date. Do not consume the item. Full refund is issued without requiring return due to health hazards.

## SECTION 3: MELTED OR DAMAGED ITEMS
Items that arrive melted (such as ice cream, chocolate, frozen foods, or baked goods) qualify for full refund. Customers do not need to return melted items as they are considered a health hazard and cannot be resold. Photographs of the melted item are required for documentation.

## SECTION 4: REFUND ELIGIBILITY
- Items spoiled upon arrival: Full refund without return
- Items damaged during shipping: Full refund without return
- Items that arrived melted: Full refund without return
- Perishable items with quality issues: Full refund without return
- Items consumed past expiration: No refund
- Items improperly stored after delivery: No refund
- Change of mind returns on perishables: Not accepted for food safety reasons

## SECTION 5: REPORTING DEADLINES
- Visible damage/spoilage: within 24 hours of delivery
- Hidden quality issues: within 48 hours of delivery
- Frozen/thawed items: within 2 hours of delivery
- Melted items: within 2 hours of delivery

## SECTION 6: DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
- Photographs of the item clearly showing the issue
- Photographs of expiration date
- Description of the problem
- Order number
- Photos of packaging if shipping damage is visible

## SECTION 7: FOOD SAFETY COMPLIANCE
For health and safety reasons, perishable items with quality issues are never required to be returned. Customers should dispose of spoiled or melted items properly. Photographs are sufficient for refund processing. This policy complies with FDA food safety guidelines and local health regulations.
